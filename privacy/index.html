Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Overview

TaskQ is a task and calendar management application operated by Taylor Stewart LLC under the Qponent brand ("we", "us", "our"). This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use TaskQ at taskq.qponent.com.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect the information provided by your Google account, including your name, email address, and profile photo. If you opt in to SMS notifications, we also collect your mobile phone number. We store task data, calendar events, expense records, and other content you create within the application. We do not collect information from third parties or purchase data about you.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and operate the TaskQ service, including authenticating your account, storing and displaying your tasks and events, sending daily email reports via SendGrid, and sending SMS notifications via Twilio when you have opted in. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS/Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number and opt in, TaskQ may send you SMS notifications about task assignments, due date reminders, and other task-related alerts. Message frequency varies based on your task activity. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of SMS notifications at any time through the TaskQ settings panel or by replying STOP to any message. Opting out of SMS does not affect your ability to use the rest of the TaskQ service. We do not share your phone number or opt-in data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase, which provides encryption in transit and at rest. We use Google OAuth for authentication, so we never store your password. Access to your data is restricted to authenticated users within your workspace. While we implement reasonable security measures, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Cookies and Tracking

TaskQ uses browser local storage and session cookies solely for authentication and application functionality. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking services.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and associated data, please contact us at the email below and we will process your request within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

TaskQ is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Your continued use of TaskQ after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at privacy@qponent.com.
